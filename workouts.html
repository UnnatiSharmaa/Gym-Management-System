Training Splits & Nutrition Guidelines

Systematic exercise routines designed to maximize muscular hypertrophy, core strength, and metabolic conditioning, paired with balanced macronutrient meal structures.

Weekly Periodization

6-Day Muscle Group Splits

Click on any training day below to review the specific exercise routine, target reps, and rest periods.

Monday: Chest, Deltoids & Triceps

Focus: Primary horizontal/vertical presses with triceps lockout volume.

High Intensity
# Exercise Movement Target Muscle Sets × Reps Rest Interval Trainer Cue
01 Barbell Flat Bench Press Pectoralis Major 4 × 8-10 reps 90 - 120 sec Keep scapula retracted and feet firmly planted.
02 Incline Dumbbell Press Clavicular Head (Upper Chest) 4 × 10-12 reps 75 - 90 sec 30-degree incline, control lowering tempo (2s).
03 Standing Overhead Barbell Press Anterior Deltoids 3 × 8-10 reps 90 sec Engage core tight, avoid excessive lumbar arching.
04 Cable Lateral Raises Lateral Deltoids 4 × 12-15 reps 60 sec Lead with elbows slightly forward in the scapular plane.
05 Parallel Bar Bodyweight Dips Lower Chest & Triceps 3 × 10-12 reps 75 sec Lean torso slightly forward for deeper chest stretch.
06 Rope Triceps Pushdowns Triceps Brachii (Lateral Head) 4 × 12-15 reps 60 sec Spread ropes at lockout and squeeze triceps for 1s.

Tuesday: Back, Posterior Chain & Biceps

Focus: Latissimus dorsi thickness, rhomboid retraction, and elbow flexor peak.

Strength Focus
# Exercise Movement Target Muscle Sets × Reps Rest Interval Trainer Cue
01 Conventional Deadlifts Erector Spinae & Hamstrings 4 × 6-8 reps 120 - 150 sec Brace core, push the floor away, maintain neutral spine.
02 Wide-Grip Lat Pulldowns Latissimus Dorsi 4 × 10-12 reps 75 sec Drive elbows downwards towards your back pockets.
03 Bent-Over Barbell Rows Mid-Back & Rhomboids 4 × 8-10 reps 90 sec Torso at 45 degrees, pull barbell towards belly button.
04 Single-Arm Dumbbell Rows Unilateral Lats 3 × 10-12 reps 60 sec Full stretch at bottom without twisting torso.
05 EZ-Bar Biceps Curls Biceps Brachii 4 × 10-12 reps 60 sec Pin elbows to your sides, avoid momentum swings.
06 Incline Dumbbell Hammer Curls Brachialis & Forearms 3 × 12 reps 60 sec Neutral grip palms facing inward for forearm thickness.

Wednesday: Quads, Hamstrings & Calves

Focus: Compound knee flexion, hip hinging, and explosive calf power.

High Calorie Burn
# Exercise Movement Target Muscle Sets × Reps Rest Interval Trainer Cue
01 Barbell Back Squats Quadriceps & Glutes 4 × 8-10 reps 120 sec Descend below parallel with knees tracking over toes.
02 Romanian Deadlifts (RDL) Hamstrings & Gluteus 4 × 10-12 reps 90 sec Hinge at hips, push pelvis backward until hamstring stretch.
03 45-Degree Leg Press Quads & Adductors 4 × 12-15 reps 90 sec Do not lock out knees forcefully at top of the movement.
04 Walking Dumbbell Lunges Unilateral Stability 3 × 12 steps/leg 75 sec Keep chest upright and take equal 90-degree strides.
05 Lying Hamstring Leg Curls Hamstring Biceps Femoris 4 × 12-15 reps 60 sec Control eccentric lowering over 3 full seconds.
06 Standing Calf Raises Gastrocnemius 4 × 15-20 reps 45 sec Pause 1s at deep bottom stretch and 1s at top peak.

Thursday: Core Stabilization & High-Intensity Cardio

Focus: Abdominal bracing, rotational strength, and VO2 Max enhancement.

Endurance
# Exercise Movement Target Area Work / Rest Sets Trainer Cue
01 Hanging Knee / Leg Raises Lower Rectus Abdominis 12 - 15 reps 4 sets Curl pelvis upward; avoid body swing or kickbacks.
02 Russian Twists with Med Ball Internal & External Obliques 20 total twists 4 sets Keep heels elevated and rotate ribs across midline.
03 Ab-Wheel Rollouts Deep Core & Transverse Abs 10 - 12 reps 3 sets Tuck tailbone under, do not let lower back sag down.
04 Concept2 Rower Sprints Full Body Metabolic 45s sprint / 45s rest 6 rounds Drive with legs first, lean back, then pull arms to chest.
05 Battle Rope Waves & Slams Shoulders & Stamina 30s work / 30s rest 5 rounds Maintain quarter-squat athletic stance throughout.

Friday: Upper Body Volume & Sculpting

Focus: Controlled pump sets for chest, upper lats, side shoulders, and arms.

Hypertrophy
# Exercise Movement Target Muscle Sets × Reps Rest Trainer Cue
01 Incline Machine Chest Press Upper Chest 4 × 10-12 reps 75 sec Continuous tension, avoid locking out triceps.
02 Seated Cable Rows (V-Bar) Mid Trapezius & Lats 4 × 10-12 reps 75 sec Pull to navel, squeeze shoulder blades together for 1s.
03 Dumbbell Arnold Press Anterior & Side Delts 3 × 10 reps 75 sec Smooth rotation from chest up over your head.
04 Cable Face Pulls Rear Deltoids & Rotator Cuff 4 × 15 reps 60 sec Pull rope towards eye level, externally rotate wrists.
05 Preacher Curl (Machine or Bar) Biceps Short Head 3 × 12 reps 60 sec Full stretch at bottom; don't lift elbows off pad.
06 Overhead Cable Triceps Extension Triceps Long Head 3 × 12-15 reps 60 sec Keep elbows tucked forward, deep overhead stretch.

Saturday: Athletic Conditioning & Turf Drills

Focus: Sled pushes, unilateral balance, joint mobility, and stamina.

Functional
# Exercise Movement Target Zone Volume Rest Trainer Cue
01 Weighted Prowler Sled Push Quads, Glutes & Heart Rate 5 × 30-meter pushes 90 sec Drive through the balls of your feet with straight arms.
02 Kettlebell Goblet Squats Hips, Adductors & Core 4 × 12 reps 60 sec Hold bell at chest height; keep elbows inside knees.
03 Farmer's Walk (Heavy Dumbbells) Grip, Traps & Core Carry 4 × 40-meter walks 75 sec Keep shoulders retracted and steps small and steady.
04 Plyometric Box Jumps Explosive Leg Power 4 × 8 jumps 60 sec Land softly in a quarter squat, stand tall, step down.
05 Foam Rolling & Hip Mobility Drills Full Body Recovery 15 minutes continuous None Target IT bands, thoracic spine, and hip flexors.
Fuel Your Performance

Sports Nutrition & Macro Guidelines

Sample balanced daily nutritional framework recommended by PulseFit clinical dietitians.

Morning Fuel

Pre-Workout & Breakfast

Pre-Workout (6:30 AM):

1 Banana + 1 cup Black Coffee or Green Tea + 5g BCAA or a handful of almonds.

Power Breakfast (8:30 AM):

3 whole eggs or 150g low-fat Paneer bhurji + 2 slices multigrain toast or 1 bowl rolled oats with chia seeds.

Midday Recovery

Lunch & Post-Workout

Wholesome Lunch (1:30 PM):

150g grilled chicken breast or 1.5 cups yellow dal / tofu + 1 cup brown rice or 2 rotis + cucumber salad.

Evening Snack (5:00 PM):

1 scoop Whey Isolate or Sattu drink + roasted chana or 1 apple with 1 tbsp peanut butter.

Night Rest

Dinner & Hydration Target

Light Dinner (8:30 PM):

Steamed fish fillet or stir-fried soy chunks & broccoli + 1 light roti or quinoa bowl with curd.

Daily Hydration Target:

Minimum 3.5 – 4.0 Liters of fresh water distributed evenly across the waking hours.
