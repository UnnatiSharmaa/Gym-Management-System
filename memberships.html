Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining, renewals, and payments.

Is there any mandatory registration or onboarding fee?
No! At PulseFit, we believe in straightforward pricing. You only pay for your selected membership duration. There are zero hidden administrative charges or equipment maintenance fees.
Can I freeze or pause my membership during college exams or travels?
Yes. Pro Athlete members can pause their plan for up to 14 consecutive days once per cycle. Annual VIP plan holders can pause for up to 45 days with advance notification at the reception desk.
How do I renew my membership when it is about to expire?
Our system automatically sends renewal alerts 5 days and 1 day prior to your plan expiry. You can renew in person at the front desk or via your digital invoice page with instant receipt generation.
Are discounts available for college students & staff?
Yes! We offer an exclusive 15% discount on 3-month, 6-month, and Annual packages for all students and faculty upon presenting a valid student identification card.
Can I book a trial workout session before paying for a plan?
Absolutely. We provide a complimentary 1-Day Trial Pass covering the cardio deck and free weight arena. You can request your pass through our contact page.
