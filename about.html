Facility Code of Conduct & Safety Rules

Required for all members

1. Re-rack Your Weights

All dumbbells and barbell weight plates must be returned to their designated trees after use.

2. Mandatory Towel Policy

Please place a workout sweat towel on benches and equipment surfaces during workouts.

3. Appropriate Athletic Attire

Clean indoor athletic trainers and proper sportswear are strictly mandatory on the gym floor.

4. Respect Equipment & Rest Intervals

Allow fellow members to work in between sets during peak training hours.
